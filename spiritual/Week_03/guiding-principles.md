# Spiritual Portfolio: Guiding Principles (Week 3 Baseline)

## Core Guiding Principles

### 1. Integrity in Stewardship
- **Description:** Applying exactness, honesty, and continuous care in managing technical systems and digital resources.
- **Application:** Ensuring network configurations, firewall rules, and cloud infrastructure are designed with security and accountability in mind.

### 2. Continuous Revelation and Learning
- **Description:** Emphasizing iterative growth, adaptability, and line-upon-line progression in technical and personal development.
- **Application:** Approaching system architecture and troubleshooting through steady refinement and reflection.

### 3. Empowering Others through Servant Leadership
- **Description:** Using technical skills to uplift, mentor, and serve communities and team members.
- **Application:** Sharing knowledge, creating clear documentation, and building systems that serve others effectively.
