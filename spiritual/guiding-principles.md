# Spiritual Guiding Principles

---

## Introduction

As an IT professional and a disciple of Jesus Christ, my technical work is guided by faith, integrity, and a commitment to service. The following principles serve as the foundation for my academic journey, professional career, and technical stewardship.

### Principle 1: Integrity in Stewardship
* **Why It Matters:** Applying exactness, honesty, and continuous care in managing technical systems and digital resources.
* **Professional Influence:** Ensures network configurations, firewall rules, and cloud infrastructure are designed with security and accountability in mind.

### Principle 2: Continuous Revelation and Learning
* **Why It Matters:** Emphasizes iterative growth, adaptability, and line-upon-line progression in technical and personal development.
* **Professional Influence:** Encourages approaching system architecture and troubleshooting through steady refinement, prayerful reflection, and continuous iteration.

### Principle 3: Empowering Others through Servant Leadership
* **Why It Matters:** Utilizing technical skills to uplift, mentor, and serve communities and team members.
* **Professional Influence:** Focuses on sharing knowledge, creating clear documentation, and building systems that serve others effectively (Mosiah 2:17).

* **Core Principle:** Emphasizing iterative growth, adaptability, and line-upon-line progression in technical and personal development.
* **Technical Alignment & Expansion:** Utilizing Agile methodologies, automated testing pipelines, and predictive cloud auto-scaling reflects a commitment to constant improvement and line-upon-line optimization.

### Principle 3: Empowering Others through Servant Leadership
* **Core Principle:** Exercising proactive initiative and personal agency (D&C 58:26–28) rather than waiting to be commanded in professional and spiritual responsibilities.
* **Technical Alignment & Expansion:** Designing reliable cloud systems (e.g., Heritage Vault 2.1) and conducting thorough peer reviews to build solutions that empower others and foster ethical tech stewardship.
