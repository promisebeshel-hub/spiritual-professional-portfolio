# Spiritual Guiding Principles

---

## Introduction

As an IT professional and a disciple of Jesus Christ, my technical work is guided by faith, integrity, and a commitment to service. The following principles serve as the foundation for my academic journey, professional career, and technical stewardship.

  <h3>Principle 1: Integrity in Stewardship</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Why It Matters:</strong> Applying exactness, honesty, and continuous care in managing technical systems and digital resources.</li>
    <li><strong>Professional Influence:</strong> Ensures network configurations, firewall rules, and cloud infrastructure are designed with security and accountability in mind.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Principle 2: Continuous Revelation & Learning</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Why It Matters:</strong> Emphasizes iterative growth, adaptability, and line-upon-line progression in technical and personal development.</li>
    <li><strong>Professional Influence:</strong> Encourages approaching system architecture and troubleshooting through steady refinement, prayerful reflection, and continuous iteration.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Principle 3: Empowering Others Through Servant Leadership</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Why It Matters:</strong> Utilizing technical skills to uplift, mentor, and serve communities and team members.</li>
    <li><strong>Professional Influence:</strong> Focuses on sharing knowledge, creating clear documentation, and building systems that serve others effectively (Mosiah 2:17).</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>1. Stewardship & Accountability in Technical Domains</h3>
  <p><strong>Core Principle:</strong> Exactness in technical configuration is a direct expression of digital stewardship.</p>
  <p><strong>Technical Alignment:</strong> Implementing Zero Trust security, mutual TLS (mTLS), and strict network micro-segmentation ensures that sacred data and user privacy are preserved against breach or unauthorized access.</p>

  <h3>2. Continuous Iteration & Revelation</h3>
  <p><strong>Core Principle:</strong> Growth and revelation come through active engagement, execution, and continuous feedback.</p>
  <p><strong>Technical Alignment:</strong> Utilizing Agile methodologies, automated testing pipelines, and predictive cloud auto-scaling reflects a commitment to constant improvement and line-upon-line optimization.</p>

  <h3>3. Personal Agency & Servant Leadership</h3>
  <p><strong>Core Principle:</strong> Exercising proactive initiative and personal agency (D&C 58:26–28) rather than waiting to be commanded in professional and spiritual responsibilities.</p>
  <p><strong>Technical Alignment:</strong> Designing reliable cloud systems (e.g., Heritage Vault 2.1) and conducting thorough peer reviews to build solutions that empower others and foster ethical tech stewardship.</p>
