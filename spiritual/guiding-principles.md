### Task 1: Integration of Christlike Attributes & Warning Signs Matrix

#### 1. Integration of Christlike Attributes
* **Patience & Diligence (Luke 21:19):** Debugging network failures or scripting complex database migrations requires patience. Embracing "in your patience possess ye your souls" prevents hasty engineering shortcuts and encourages exactness.
* **Integrity in Stewardship (Gong, 2021):** Securing cloud data and enforcing Least Privilege access controls represents a digital covenant. Cultivating Christlike integrity ensures system policies are executed transparently and honestly.
* **Humility & Submissiveness (Alma 37:37):** Seeking divine guidance in professional decision-making ensures intellect is magnified through prayerful retrospectives and counsel.

#### 2. Personal Warning Signs & Realignment Strategies

| Warning Sign (Indicator of Drift) | Impact on Technical Work | Realignment Strategy & Gospel Principle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spiritual Inertia & Hurried Prayer:** Rushing devotions to clear ticket queues. | Reduced clarity and increased frustration during debugging. | **Daily Quiet Hours:** Isolate dedicated quiet time for prayer prior to starting terminal sessions (Nelson, 2021). |
| **Perfectionism & Self-Reliance:** Attempting to solve major outages without seeking counsel. | Burnout, isolation, and delayed project timelines. | **Line-upon-Line Iteration:** Embrace incremental growth (2 Nephi 28:30) and seek early peer feedback. |
